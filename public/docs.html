Section 1

Executive Overview

Hospital Workforce Administration & AI Vision Surveillance Platform

K-Duracare is an enterprise healthcare workforce management and operational intelligence platform designed specifically for Kanakadurga Nursing Home (A Unit of Dr K B Chowdary Healthcare Providers). The system unifies hospital staffing directory management, biometric muster rolls, shift rosters, NABH compliance tracking, automated payroll calculations, and real-time computer vision camera surveillance.

System Architecture & Technology Stack

Technical infrastructure, React core structure, and state management flow

Frontend Layer

  • Framework: React 18 + Vite 8
  • Routing: React Router v7 (Layout & ProtectedRoutes)
  • Styling Engine: Tailwind CSS v4 + Autoprefixer
  • UI Components & Icons: Lucide React Icons, Custom Badges, Toast
  • Data Visualization: Recharts 3.x (Bar Charts, Area, Pie)

Context & Security Architecture

  • Authentication Context: AuthContext.jsx (Local persistence + Demo Switcher)
  • Access Control: ProtectedRoute Guard & Permission Matrix
  • Clinical Guard: canAccessPatient() & Ward Data Scope
  • AI Intelligence Engine: Google Gemini 2.5 Flash API + Mock Fallback

Section 4

Core Modules & Feature Breakdown

Detailed guide to every workspace view in K-Duracare

1. Dashboard Portal (/dashboard)

Multi-Role Router

Dynamically renders customized dashboard widgets based on logged-in user role:

  • Doctor Dashboard: Physician patient roster, ICU telemetry alert feed, consult queue.
  • Nurse Dashboard: Ward bed occupancy, medication administration timers, vitals log.
  • Operations Dashboard: Staff attendance rates, CCTV camera alerts, department coverage.
  • Employee Self-Service: Punch status, leave balances, duty roster card.

2. Workforce Directory & Employee 360

/employees & /employees/:id

Searchable staff roster with category filters (Medical, Administrative, Support), shift tags, biometric IDs, and individual 360 profile pages featuring AI monthly attendance summaries.

3. Attendance & Biometric Muster

/attendance

Real-time biometric punch sync, muster roll reconciliation, missing punch regularization review, late entry tracking, and overtime hour logs.

4. Shifts, Rosters & NABH Coverage

/shifts

Shift master (Morning 07-14, Evening 13-20, Night 19-08, General 09-17:30), weekly departmental duty matrix, and NABH nurse-to-patient ratio compliance indicators.

5. Leave Portal & Impact Analysis

/leave

Leave application workflow (Casual, Sick, Earned, Maternity), automated AI staffing impact analyzer preventing department understaffing (<80% coverage warnings), and hospital leave calendar.

6. Payroll & Financial Processing

/payroll

Base salary band configuration, PF/ESI/TDS tax deductions, Loss of Pay (LOP) calculations, overtime payout register, AI payroll anomaly detector, and downloadable PDF-styled payslips.

Section 5

AI CCTV Surveillance & Computer Vision

Automated threat detection, worker posture tracking, and hygiene turnaround analytics

Camera Network Telemetry (/monitor)

Monitors 12 continuous CCTV camera feeds covering critical hospital zones: ICU Beds, Emergency Ramp, Operation Theatre Entrance, Pathology Lab, Canteen, and Main Security Gates.

🟢 CAM-ICU-01 (Online - 30 FPS)
🟢 CAM-OPD-01 (Online - 30 FPS)
🟡 CAM-ICU-03 (Degraded - Low FPS)
🔴 CAM-OT-01 (Offline - Maintenance Required)

Computer Vision Features (/activity)

  • Work-Zone Pose Analysis: Distinguishes bedside active duty, sitting at vitals terminal, transit walking, and canteen break presence.
  • Break Duration Sensor: Enforces 30-minute break limit across canteen and pantry zones.
  • Fall Detection & Safety Alerts: High-confidence pose vector fall alerts in patient zones with instant supervisor alerts.
  • Proximity & Group Interactions: Tracks staff interaction duration to distinguish quick clinical handovers from prolonged non-work idle conversations.
